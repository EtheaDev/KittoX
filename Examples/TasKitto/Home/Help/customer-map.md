# Customer map

The customers on a **map**, placed by their address, rather than in a list.

It is useful for the question a list cannot answer - *who else is near the customer I am visiting
on Thursday?* - and it shows nothing a customer record does not already hold: the pin is the
address, and clicking it opens the customer.

A customer whose address cannot be placed does not appear on the map; check the address on the
[customer](customers) itself.
