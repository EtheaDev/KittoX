# Lists and forms

Most screens of TasKitto are **lists**, and the same gestures work on all of them.

## The search panel

Above the rows, a **Search panel** narrows what is shown, with the filters that screen offers -
a date range, a customer, a status, a free text box. Filters add up; emptying one puts back the
rows it was hiding.

## The toolbar

**New**, **Edit**, **View**, **Delete**, **Refresh**. A record other records depend on cannot be
deleted - a project with phases, a phase with activities - and the application says what is
holding it.

## Sorting, paging, exporting

Click a heading to sort, click again to reverse. Long lists are read a page at a time. Several
screens offer **Download in CSV**, **Download in Excel** or **Download in TXT**, and what comes
out is what the list is showing, filters included.

## Tabs under a form

A record that owns others shows them in a tab under its form - the phases of a project, for
instance - with a toolbar of their own. They are saved together with the record they belong to.
