# Employees, roles and activity types

The three short lists that the rest of the application chooses from. They are under **Tables**,
and they are worth filling in before recording activities, because an activity asks for all three.

## Employees

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Employee Name** | Required. |
| **Employee Type** | What kind of employee. |

## Operator roles

The role an employee acts in on a given activity - the same person may be an analyst on one
activity and a tester on the next, which is why the role is on the activity and not on the
employee.

## Activity types

What kind of work an activity is. It is the list the [activities by type](activities-by-type)
screen groups by, and the one the type pie of the [dashboard](dashboard) counts.

## Before deleting

None of the three can be deleted while an activity refers to it.
