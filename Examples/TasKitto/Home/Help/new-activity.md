# New Activity Wizard

The way to record an activity when you would rather be asked than face a form with ten fields on
it. **Activities → New Activity** opens a wizard of three steps, with **Back** and **Next** at
the bottom.

## Step 1 - Project & Phase

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | The phase of the project the work belongs to. Required - and it is what ties the activity to a project and therefore to a customer. |
| **Employee** | Who did the work. Required. |
| **Role** | In which role. Required. |

## Step 2 - Activity Details

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | What was done. Required. |
| **Type** | One of the activity types - the short list under **Tables**. Required. |
| **Status** | Where the activity stands. |

## Step 3 - Date & Time

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Activity Date** | The day. Required. |
| **Start Time**, **End Time** | The hours. The duration is worked out from them. |

The last button is **Create Activity**: until it is pressed nothing is written, and **Back** can
still change any answer. The same activity can be recorded from the
[input list](activity-input) instead, with everything on one form - the wizard is for the times
you are not sure what comes next.
