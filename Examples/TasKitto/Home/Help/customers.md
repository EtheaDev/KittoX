# Customers

Who the work is done for.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Customer Name** | Required. |
| **Address**, **City** | Where they are. The city is also what puts them on the [map](customer-map). |
| **Phone**, **Email** | How to reach them. |
| **Status** | Where the relationship stands. |

A customer cannot be deleted while projects hang from it: see [projects and
phases](projects-and-phases).
