# Users and permissions

Under **Maintenance**, three lists decide who may sign in and what they may see.

- **Users** - who exists, with their user name.
- **User roles** - which roles a user has.
- **Permissions** - what a role may do.

Permissions are granted to **roles**, and roles to **users**: nobody is given a permission
directly. That is what keeps "what may a sales person do?" a question with one answer, instead of
one answer per person.

## Your own password

**Change password** asks for the one you have and for the new one, twice. An empty password is
never accepted. If the application asked you to change it as soon as you signed in, the one you
used was temporary: it works once, to let you choose your own.

**Logout** ends the session. A session also ends by itself after a while without activity, and
what was not saved at that point is lost.
