# Activity dashboard

**Statistics → Dashboard** puts on one screen the numbers the lists would make you count by hand.

| Panel | |
|---|---|
| **KPI - activities** | How many activities. |
| **KPI - hours** | How many hours they add up to. |
| **KPI - projects** | How many projects they are spread over. |
| **KPI - customers** | How many customers those projects belong to. |
| **Status pie** | The activities by the status they are in. |
| **Type pie** | The activities by their type - the same split as [activities by type](activities-by-type), counted instead of listed. |
| **Daily chart** | The activities day by day. |

Everything on it is read from the same activities the [input list](activity-input) shows: it is a
reading of that table, not a second one. The two pie charts also exist as screens of their own
under **Statistics**, together with a bar chart, for when one of them is all you want to look at.
