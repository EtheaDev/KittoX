# Your account

## Changing your password

**User → Change password** asks for the password you have now and for the new one, twice. The new
password cannot be empty: an empty password is never accepted, which is why "leave it blank" is
not a way to remove it.

If the application asked you to change your password as soon as you signed in, it is because the
one you used was a temporary one: it works once, to let you choose your own.

## Closing the session

**User → Logout** ends the session and brings back the sign-in page. A session also ends by
itself after a while without activity - what you had not saved at that point is lost, which is
worth knowing before leaving a form open over lunch.
