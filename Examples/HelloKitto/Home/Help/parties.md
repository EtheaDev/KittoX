# Parties

A party has a date, a place, and the girls who were invited to it.

## Party list

The search panel is called **Search Parties**. The toolbar carries, besides the usual buttons,
**Download in CSV** and **Download in Excel**: both take away what the list is showing, filters
included.

## What a party is made of

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Party Name** | The name of the party. Required. |
| **Party Date** | The day. Required. |
| **Party Time** | The hour. Required. |
| **Address** | Where it is held. Required. |
| **Period** | A free label - "morning", "afternoon" - used to group the parties. |

## Invitations

Open a party and you will find its **invitations** in a tab under the form. Each one is a girl and
a yes or no:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Invitee** | The [girl](girls) invited. Required. |
| **Accepted** | Whether she said yes. |

The same girl cannot be invited twice to the same party: the application refuses the second
invitation and says so. Invitations are saved together with the party they belong to - adding one
and then closing the form without saving leaves nothing behind.

## Party calendar

**Parties → Party calendar** puts the same parties on a calendar, by their date. It answers the
question a list answers badly - *what is happening that week?* - and nothing else: it is the same
parties, seen by date.
