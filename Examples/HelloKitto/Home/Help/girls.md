# Girls

The girls are the people of HelloKitto: a doll belongs to one of them, and an invitation is sent
to one of them.

## Girl list

Opens on every girl in the database. The search panel is called **Girls Filter** and offers a row
of buttons under **Hair Color** - Blond, Walnut, Black, Silver, Red, Platinum. Pressing one leaves
the girls with that hair; pressing it again puts the others back.

## What a girl is made of

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name of the girl. Required. It is the name the other screens show her by. |
| **Age** | Her age, in years. Required. |
| **Hair** | One of the [hair types](hair-types). Required. |
| **Phone** | A phone number. Optional. |

## Adding a girl

**Girls → Girl form** opens an empty form - the same one the **New** button of the list opens.
Fill in at least name, age and hair, then save.

## What holds a girl back

A girl cannot be deleted while she is the mom of a doll or the invitee of a party: delete or
reassign those first. The application names what is holding her.
