# Projects

What the company is working on.

## What a project is made of

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Proj id** | The code, up to five characters. It is the key. |
| **Proj name** | The name. Required. |
| **Product** | The product the project belongs to. |
| **Team Leader** | The [employee](employees) who leads it. |
| **Proj desc** | The description, as long as you need. |

## Who works on it, and on whose budget

Two things connect a project to the rest of the company, and they are worth knowing even though
each is a table of its own:

- **Employee projects** says which employees work on which projects. The same employee may be on
  several, and a project has several people on it.
- **Project department budgets** says, for a fiscal year, how much a **department** put into a
  **project** and with how many people: the key is the three of them together - year, department,
  project - which is what lets two departments fund the same project in the same year.

## Before deleting one

A project with people on it, or with a budget recorded against it, cannot be deleted.
