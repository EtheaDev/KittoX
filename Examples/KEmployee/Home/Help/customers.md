# Customers

Who buys from the company, and what they ordered.

## What a customer is made of

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Customer Id** | The number that identifies the customer. |
| **Customer Name** | Required. |
| **Contact Firstname**, **Contact Lastname** | The person to talk to. |
| **Phone no**, **City**, **State/Province** | Where and how to reach them. |

## Orders

Open a customer and the tab underneath holds **their orders**:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **PO number** | The order number. It is the key. |
| **Order status** | Where the order stands. Required. |
| **Order date** | When it was placed. Required. |
| **Ship date**, **Date needed** | When it left, and when it was wanted. |
| **Qty ordered**, **Total value**, **Discount** | How much, for how much, less how much. |
| **Item type** | What kind of goods. |
| **Paid** | Whether it was paid. |

Orders are saved together with the customer they belong to.

## Exporting

The toolbar carries **Download in CSV** and **Download in Excel**: what comes out is the customers
the list is showing, filters included.
