# Countries

The shortest list in the application, and the one the others lean on.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Country** | The name of the country. It is the key: it is what [jobs](jobs) and customers refer to. |
| **Currency** | The currency of that country. Required. |

The currency is what makes the salary range of a job mean something: a minimum of 20.000 is not
the same number in two countries, and the country is what says which.

## Before deleting one

A country used by a job or by a customer cannot be deleted.
